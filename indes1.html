Research Interests

My research focuses on computational modeling of polymers using molecular dynamics (MD) simulations, with a specialization in cross-linked polymer networks and hydrogels. I utilize LAMMPS and bead-spring models to explore nanoscale mechanical and thermal properties, including elasticity, plasticity, friction, and lubrication.

Current projects involve nanotribology of hydrogels, analyzing how molecular architecture and cross-linking density affect frictional forces, stiffness, and structural properties. Collaboration with experimental groups complements the computational insights, using AFM, tribometers, and rheometers for validation.
